How selection works

The gallery chooses an eligible record at random. Within that record, featured datasets take priority, followed by the highest rated; ties are random.

  • All previewable records: Uses the first preview if none is featured or rated.
  • Featured or rated records: Requires a featured dataset or one meeting the minimum rating.
  • Featured only: Includes only featured datasets.
